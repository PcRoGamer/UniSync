# Acceptable use

**echo360-dlp** is for **personal study only**. It does not grant access to content you cannot already view on Echo360.

## What this tool is for

If you are enrolled and Echo360 lets you watch a lecture, you are generally already allowed to use that recording for your own study. On Echo360 itself, getting everything offline is **manual and tedious**:

- **Video:** usually one lecture at a time through the web UI
- **Transcript:** separate from the video — another download, another step
- **Using both together:** you combine the MP4 and transcript yourself in a player or editor

**echo360-dlp automates that:** batch download for a whole course, `.vtt` transcripts saved alongside each lecture, optional soft subs muxed into the MP4, consistent folders (`mp4/`, `transcript/`). Same content you could reach in the browser; far less clicking. It is a convenience tool, not a way to access material you are not entitled to.

**Only use it for courses and lectures you are authorised to access** through your institution’s Echo360 account.

## Allowed

- Downloading lectures you can already open on Echo360 (enrolled / authorised access)
- Keeping copies on your own devices for **private** review, note-taking, and revision
- Watching offline or with your own player — the same personal use as on Echo360, without redoing manual video + transcript downloads each week

## Not allowed

You **must not** use this tool to obtain content and then:

- Share, upload, or redistribute lecture recordings (Discord, Google Drive, YouTube, etc.)
- Give copies to other students who do not have access through Echo360
- Publish or sell downloaded media in any form
- Download content for courses you are not enrolled in or not permitted to view

**Redistribution of downloaded lectures is prohibited.** This tool is not for distribution, mirroring, or publishing course content.

## Your responsibilities

- Comply with your **university's** academic integrity rules and policies
- Comply with **Echo360's** terms of service and any licence on the recordings
- Respect copyright and the rights of lecturers and institutions
